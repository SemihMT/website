About Me

Background

I'm a graduate of Digital Arts & Entertainment where I studied game development with a focus on modern C++, graphics programming, and tool development. Since graduating I've been teaching Python and computer science to high school students, and serving as Regional Coordinator for vzw Jet across East and West Flanders, leading events, youth camps, and career mentoring for hundreds of young people.

Those experiences have made me a better communicator and a more patient problem solver. They've also made it clearer that building software is where I want to be.

What I'm After

My instinct as a developer is to go deeper, to understand how systems fit together rather than just making them work. That's what draws me to engine and tool development: building the things that let other developers do their best work.

I don't have decades of engine credits to my name yet, but I have a solid foundation in graphics programming, a completed networking library, and a genuine interest in systems design that I'm actively building on.

Get In Touch

If you're working on something interesting in the engine, tools, or gameplay space, I'd love to hear about it.

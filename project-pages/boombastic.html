Group Project

BoomBastic: Calavera Carnaval

2024 · Group project · Unity / C#
itch.io ↗
C# Unity Group School

Overview

BoomBastic is a couch co-op party game for up to four players, developed during the Game Projects course. Set in a Day of the Dead inspired graveyard, players pass and throw an exploding piñata bomb between each other. The last skeleton standing when it detonates wins. Rounds are short, chaotic, and designed for quick, replayable fun.

The concept started as a simple "hot potato" brainstorming idea. We wanted something immediately understandable, physically expressive, and fun to play even with people who had never touched the game before. After pitching the concept and getting approval from our team leads, we developed and shipped the project over the course of five months.

My Contributions

  • The bomb: Implemented custom physics behaviour for the bomb when thrown, including the arc, spin, and bounce behaviour. The goal was to keep the gameplay chaotic and unpredictable while still feeling fair and controllable.
  • Dash mechanic: Built the dash system that allows players to quickly evade the bomb when cornered, including cooldown handling, directional input, and a short invincibility window during the dodge.
  • World pickups & traps: Implemented ofrenda altar pickups that replenish dashes, as well as floor traps that slow players down. Both systems include visual feedback and tuned timing to support the game's pacing.
  • Scene management: Implemented scene transitions and the dynamic end-screen logic that determines the winner and displays the correct result for each player.
